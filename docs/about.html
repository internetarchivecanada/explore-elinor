The data

Every record lives in the public Zotero group Wiki ReCite, in the collection "Elinor - under construction": Wiki ReCite on Zotero ↗

The site starts from English Wikipedia articles, plus works found on archive.org that no article cites yet.

What "Archive copy" means

A Wayback Machine snapshot or an archive.org item is attached to the Zotero record. Whether each original link still works has not been checked yet.

"No copy found" means an archive pass looked and found none. "Not checked yet" means no one has looked: so far only the Elinor Ostrom citations have been through an archive pass.

Records flagged "Unprocessed" haven't been cleaned up yet: their type or details may be wrong.
